Subject Themes in Ukraine's History

How much publishing attention seven of the DK508 (Ukraine — History) catalog's own subject themes — not places — have received, year by year from 1727 to 2016. Unlike the companion Russia/Poland river, this chart tracks when books were published about each theme, not when the underlying events happened — see the methodology note for why, and how that changes what the shape means.

What this measures: for each year, how many distinct records whose subject headings match one of seven hand-picked themes — Politics and government, Foreign relations, Antiquities, Nationalism & independence movements, World War II, Revolution & Civil War (1917–21), Famine (Holodomor) — were published that year (the same LC call-number year used throughout this project), summed across — records. These seven were chosen from the collection's actual most frequent non-geographic heading segments, keeping only substantive historical/political themes and excluding cataloging form subdivisions that rank just as high in the raw counts — Biography, Congresses, Pictorial works, Dictionaries, Catalogs — which describe what kind of book it is, not what it's about. A record can count under more than one theme.

Why publication year, not event year: the companion Russia/Poland river plots each mention by the year its OWN subject-heading date subdivision covers (e.g. a heading dated "1917–1921" counts toward those exact years) — but topical headings like "Nationalism" or "Antiquities" carry no date of their own to borrow, and even the three themes named after a specific episode overwhelmingly appear in books published decades after that episode, about it, not from it: this catalog has no book physically published during the 1932–33 famine, for instance. So every band on this chart is dated the only way that's actually possible here — by when the book itself came out — which makes this a chart of historiography (when people wrote about a theme), not of the theme's own chronology. The flowing curve is a —-year centered moving average of the raw yearly counts, smoothed for legibility (even the largest band averages under 4 records/year across 290 years); hover the chart, or switch to the table, for the exact unsmoothed number behind any point.

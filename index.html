VICOLEX (Visual Collection Explorer): Mapping Ukraine's History Through a Library Catalog

VICOLEX turns more than 9,000 Library of Congress catalog records on Ukrainian history (class DK508) into interactive maps, timelines and networks. It grew out of the VICOLEX prototype described in Buchel and Sedig, "Making sense of document collections with map-based visualisations" (Information Research, 2014).

Views

  • Morphing contour maps of Ukraine's lands, 5050 BCE to today: 43 historical maps, with regions sliding from one map to the next, and the events, people and records linked to each period.
  • Historical maps with traced contours
  • Historical maps, 5050 BCE to today
  • Map and network of books about Ukraine
  • Influence river: whose writing shapes the record, over time
  • Subject themes over time
  • Publication delay after historical events, and delay by language
  • Time search
  • 3D relief and homogeneity map
  • Lifepaths in motion: the people of the catalog across the world, including the diaspora

Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk are shown as territory of Ukraine unlawfully occupied by Russia. Many of the older map reconstructions were made with the help of AI and are approximate.
